## Overview

A placeholder case study. Replace this with a real project.

This was a product design engagement for a fintech company — redesigning their core transaction flow from scratch.

## Problem

Users were dropping off at the confirmation step. The existing flow had too many steps and unclear hierarchy.

## Solution

Consolidated five screens into two. Made the confirmation state feel like a moment of arrival, not a formality.

## Results

Drop-off decreased by 34% in the first month after launch.
