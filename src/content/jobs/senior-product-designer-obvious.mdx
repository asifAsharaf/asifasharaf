Obvious is looking for a Senior Product Designer to join their growing team in Bengaluru. You'll be working across multiple product verticals, owning design from research through to shipped features.

## What you'll do

- Own design for one or more product areas end-to-end
- Work closely with engineering and product to ship high-quality experiences
- Contribute to the Obvious design system
- Mentor junior designers on the team

## What they're looking for

- 4+ years of product design experience
- Strong visual and interaction design fundamentals
- Experience with complex, data-heavy UI
- Comfortable with ambiguity and rapid iteration
